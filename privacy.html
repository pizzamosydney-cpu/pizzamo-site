Privacy policy

PizzAmò is run by Salvatore Cerreto (ABN 92 337 664 922), 161 Parramatta Rd, Homebush NSW 2140. This page explains what personal information we collect through this website and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email, phone number (if you give it) and your message.
  • Website statistics: we use Google Analytics to see how many people visit and which pages they use. It collects information such as your approximate location, device and browser, and uses cookies to do so. It does not tell us who you are.

Online orders

When online ordering is available, orders are placed on our ordering partner's website, linked from this site. You give your details and payment information to them, not to this website, and their own privacy policy applies. We receive the details needed to make and hand over your order.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your message, handle your order and improve the website. We don't sell your information or use it for marketing unless you have asked to hear from us.

Who else sees it

Messages from the contact form are stored by Netlify, which hosts this website, and sent to our email inbox (Gmail, run by Google). Website statistics are processed by Google. These services may store data outside Australia. We don't share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies to count visits. The map on our home page comes from Google Maps and may set Google's own cookies when it loads. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address but sets no cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and the site will still work.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Use the contact form on our home page.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
