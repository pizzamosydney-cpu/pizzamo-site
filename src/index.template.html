Good to know

Questions

Do you have gluten-free or vegan options?

Yes. Any pizza can be made on a gluten-free base (+$5) or with dairy-free cheese (+$3). Vegetarian pizzas are marked V on the menu.

Can you cater my event?

Not from the truck. For events, contact Basilico Pizza Catering.

About

PizzAmò, by Basilico.

Basilico is one of Sydney's most popular pizza catering businesses, bringing authentic Neapolitan pizza to homes and events for over three successful years. As a full-time catering business, we dedicate our week to preparing homemade ingredients, refining our recipes and caring for every detail. From private parties to corporate celebrations, we serve freshly baked pizza with warm Italian hospitality.
